Kept

Your Font Book, sorted.

Kept reads the fonts installed on your Mac, the same ones Font Book shows, and puts them on shelves: serif, sans-serif, mono, display and script. System and language fonts go out of the way. Nothing is uploaded; your fonts stay on your computer.

This browser can’t see installed fonts.

Open Kept in Chrome or Edge on your Mac to bring in your whole Font Book at once. Here, you can still add font files below.
