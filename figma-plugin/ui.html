Kept
How it works

Your Kept fonts, in Figma.

Bring in the fonts and pairs you saved on the Kept website, then use them on your designs.

  1. Download a backup from KeptOn the Kept website, open Library. Next to Rescan, click Backup → Download a backup. It saves a file named kept-backup-(date).json.
  2. Load it hereClick Load from Kept at the top and choose that file. Your saved fonts, pairs and notes appear under Pairs and Fonts.
  3. Use it on your designSelect text layers, then choose Apply to selection on a pair. Auto gives the largest text the heading font, the smallest the caption font, and the rest the body font.
No backup yet?
You can start here: bookmark fonts under Fonts → All in Figma, and make pairs with New pair.
Loaded a backup before?
Loading a newer one only adds what’s new. Nothing you saved here is replaced.
“Not available in Figma”?
That font isn’t installed where Figma can see it. Install it on this computer, then restart the Figma desktop app.
Where is my data kept?
In Figma, on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, and the plugin never changes your font files.
Changed anything in Kept?

Bring it over with a fresh backup: in Kept, Library → Backup → Download a backup, then load it here.
